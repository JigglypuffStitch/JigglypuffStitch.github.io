About Me

Hi! I am a master's student in Electrical Engineering at Stanford University, where I work with Prof. Serena Yeung-Levy at the MARVL Lab and Prof. Ehsan Adeli at the STAI Lab.

Previously, I was a research intern at Microsoft Research Asia (MSRA), working with Dr. Fangzhao Wu on LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning. I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Wuhan University.

My research interests lie in AI agents and their real-world applications, with a particular interest in AI for Biology (AI4Bio). I am interested in developing AI agents that can understand complex scientific information, reason over experimental evidence, and contribute to biological research and scientific discovery.

Publications

BioThread: Tracing Claim–Evidence Reasoning in Biomedical Scientific Figures

Chenyue Li, Xiaoxiao Sun, Huangqingbo Sun, Yuwei Yang, Elaine Sui, Qinlin Zhao, Emma Lundberg, Serena Yeung-Levy

Submitted to CVPR 2027

We introduce BioThread to evaluate whether vision-language models can interpret biomedical experimental figures, integrate evidence across experiments, and draw scientifically supported conclusions. We further develop an evidence-grounded agent to improve scientific reasoning, with the broader goal of enabling AI agents to support biological research.

ScreenHaystack: Finding Blind Zones in GUI Grounding

Chenyue Li, Xiaoxiao Sun, Yubo Deng, Qinlin Zhao, Serena Yeung-Levy, Yuhui Zhang

EMNLP 2026, Main Conference

Paper/arXiv

We introduce a dynamic benchmark to investigate the spatial reliability of GUI grounding models. We identify systematic spatial blind zones in existing models and propose a targeted data augmentation strategy to improve grounding performance.

Reasoning Improves LLMs’ Stability in Decision Making

Dacheng Wen*, Chenyue Li*, Chenglong Wang, Xiaoyuan Yi, Jing Yao, Mingxiao An, Bin Zhu, Wei Chen, Lingjuan Lyu, Junming Huang, Xing Xie, Bu Zhong, Francis C. M. Lau, Yupeng Li, Fangzhao Wu

Submitted to Nature Communications

We investigate the role of reasoning in improving the stability of LLM decision-making across ethical and professional domains. Our findings demonstrate that reasoning can improve decision consistency independently of accuracy gains.

Transformer for Object Re-Identification: A Survey

Mang Ye, Shuoyi Chen, Chenyue Li, Wei-Shi Zheng, David Crandall, Bo Du

International Journal of Computer Vision (IJCV), 2025

Paper/Code

We provide a comprehensive review of Transformer-based object re-identification across different modalities and learning settings, establish new experimental benchmarks, and discuss open challenges in the foundation model era.

Adaptive High-Frequency Transformer for Diverse Wildlife Re-Identification

Chenyue Li, Shuoyi Chen, Mang Ye

ECCV 2024

Paper/Code

We propose an adaptive high-frequency Transformer for wildlife re-identification, leveraging discriminative visual features to recognize individual animals across diverse species.
